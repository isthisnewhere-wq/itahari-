Find your kind of latte.

A quick guide to Cafe Latte, Honey Latte, and the three supplied flavour options.

A latte generally combines espresso with steamed milk and a light layer of foam. If you enjoy a milk-forward coffee, it is a good starting point for comparing the drinks on Bahattar Cafe’s menu.

Begin with Cafe Latte

The listed Cafe Latte costs Rs. 165. It lets you begin with the espresso-and-milk combination before choosing a flavoured version. Exact milk options and cup sizes are not listed, so ask the café if you have a preference or dietary requirement.

Vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut?

Flavour Latte is listed at Rs. 265, with Vanilla, Caramel, and Hazelnut options. Vanilla commonly adds a soft, sweet aroma; caramel brings a toasted-sugar character; hazelnut gives a nutty flavour. These are general flavour descriptions, rather than a claim about a particular syrup or recipe.

Honey Latte is a separate menu item at Rs. 275. If you are deciding between it and Flavour Latte, ask how sweet each is and whether the café can adjust it.

Prefer it cold?

Flavour Iced Latte costs Rs. 295 and offers Vanilla, Hazelnut, and Caramel. Iced Cappuccino or Iced Latte is listed separately at Rs. 245. The menu also lists Add Flavour at Rs. 100; confirm how that add-on applies before ordering rather than assuming it is already included in every drink.

For allergies, ask about ingredients and shared preparation equipment. A flavour name alone does not tell you whether a drink contains an allergen.
