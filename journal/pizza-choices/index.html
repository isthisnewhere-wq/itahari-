A pizza for your table.

Compare the exact ingredients and prices supplied for Chicken Pizza, Meat Lover, and Veggie Lover.

When choosing a pizza, compare the toppings first and then the price. Bahattar Cafe lists three pizzas. Sizes, slice counts, and customisations have not been supplied, so check those details before deciding how many to order.

Chicken Pizza

Chicken Pizza is Rs. 705. The supplied description is “Chicken, mozzarella cheese, vegetables, and pilati tomato”. It includes both chicken and vegetables, with mozzarella named as the cheese.

Meat Lover

Meat Lover is Rs. 795. Its supplied ingredients are “Bacon, grilled chicken, pork, mozzarella cheese, and pilati tomato”. Choose it only if the listed meats suit everyone sharing the pizza. Ask the café about any ingredient questions.

Veggie Lover

Veggie Lover is Rs. 615. The supplied description is “Seasonal vegetables, mozzarella cheese, and pilati tomato”. The vegetables may depend on the café’s current preparation, so ask what is included. Because mozzarella is listed, this description does not support calling the pizza vegan.

Before you share

Confirm the pizza size, portion expectations, availability, and any dietary restrictions. The phrase “pilati tomato” is preserved from the supplied menu; ask the café to clarify it if you need ingredient details.
