Something cool, please.

Lassi, smoothies, iced tea, and refreshers: find a non-coffee drink that fits your taste.

If you want a cold drink without coffee, start with texture and flavour. A lassi, a smoothie, and a refresher can feel quite different. Bahattar Cafe lists each as a separate option, along with iced tea and lemonade.

Lassi for a yogurt-based style

Lassi is generally a yogurt-based drink. Sweet Lassi or Plain Lassi is listed at Rs. 155, while Banana Lassi costs Rs. 175. Ask about the café’s ingredients, sweetness, and dairy content before ordering if these affect your choice.

Smoothies for a blended drink

Flavour Smoothies are Rs. 265, with Kiwi, Strawberry, Blueberry, and Mango options. The flavour list does not establish whether the café uses fresh fruit, purée, syrup, dairy, or another base. Ask if you need a particular ingredient or preparation.

Refreshers, lemonade, and iced tea

Flavour Refreshers cost Rs. 275 and offer Kiwi, Blueberry, Orange, Peach, and Mango. Lemonade is Rs. 215; Mint Lemonade is Rs. 250. Iced Tea is Rs. 200, with Peach, Lemon, and Apple options. Choose by flavour, then ask about sweetness and serving size.

The supplied menu names drinks, not nutritional values. It would be misleading to describe an option as sugar-free, dairy-free, or a fresh juice without checking its recipe.
