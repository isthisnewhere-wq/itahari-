# Bahattar design system

The requested nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill repository was cloned into `.design-tools/ui-ux-pro-max-skill`. Both the installed guidance and the cloned search script were consulted. The coffee-shop query returned a relevant premium dark / gold palette and Cormorant / Montserrat typography direction, but its Liquid Glass style recommendation was a poor fit for the supplied request. The first query also returned an overly vibrant style. Those effects are deliberately not used. The selected design follows the user's restrained direction and the guidance on food photography, visible hours, keyboard support, contrast, and reduced motion.

- Warm paper `#f6f3eb`, espresso / forest ink `#252b26`, muted olive `#667049`, warm brown `#8b5437`.
- System Georgia display serif, Arial body text, and monospace annotation labels. No remote font requests or font layout shifts.
- Large editorial headings, thin borders, quiet technical annotations, an architectural photo arch, and a fine line rosette. No continuous animation or neon effects.
- Home: asymmetrical photo and stacked serif heading. Coffee: offset italic heading and arched coffee image. Food: sans headline on dark green with plated food. Breakfast: centered sun illustration and warm background. Menu: open editorial heading with count, search, and pill filters.
- Secondary pages use a mix of photo splits, practical tables, document layouts, and concise journal layouts.
- All photos are supplied WebP originals, about 140–166 KB each. No stock photo is presented as café photography. The food photo is not assigned to an unconfirmed specific menu item.
- Explicit image dimensions, lazy loading below the hero, visible focus rings, 44px main controls, semantic landmarks, and reduced-motion support.
